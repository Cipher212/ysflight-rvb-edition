Preparation: average stereo channels, remove DC, make a 200 ms equal-power
overlap join, remove residual DC, normalize to -14 dBFS RMS with -1 dBFS peak
ceiling, save PCM16 WAV. Output peaks are approximately -6.99 and -6.63 dBFS.
Both loops are shared resources, including the afterburner voice. Pitch and
mix envelopes are still controlled by the game. Extra cockpit muffling uses
the existing PlayerEngine filter; it is not baked irreversibly into the WAV.

Reproduce with tools/prepare_jet_audio.py and the two original downloads in
ignore/replays/sound. Python dependencies (offline developer tooling only):
numpy and soundfile. Neither original download nor the converter dependencies
are required to play the game. File regions, join and normalization settings
are constants in the preparation tool. WAVs load directly without import cache.
